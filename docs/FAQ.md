# FAQ

### What is this repo?

A curated, regularly-updated collection of **Seedance 2.0** AI video prompts. Each entry shows the prompt, its output (video thumbnail / image), the creator, and the original source post. Browse the full, playable gallery on **[gptproto.com](https://gptproto.com)**.

### What is Seedance 2.0?

An AI video generation model that turns a text prompt (text-to-video) or a starting image (image-to-video) into short cinematic clips. You can generate Seedance 2.0 videos via the [gptproto API](https://gptproto.com).

### Why are the videos shown as images?

GitHub READMEs can't play video inline, so each card shows the video's poster frame. Click any thumbnail to watch it play on the gallery.

### How often is the list updated?

The README is regenerated from the gptproto materials library. New trending prompts are added as they're curated — watch the **News** section and ⭐ star the repo to follow along.

### Can I use these prompts?

Yes. Content is released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) — reuse freely with attribution. Prompts are aggregated from public posts and credited to their authors.

### How do I add a prompt?

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) — open a prompt submission issue.

### I'm the author of a clip and want it changed/removed.

Open an issue and we'll take care of it.
